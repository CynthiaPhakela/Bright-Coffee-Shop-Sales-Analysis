# Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis – Business Intelligence Case Study

## Business Objective

The primary objective of this case study was to help Bright Coffee Shop understand its sales performance by identifying the products generating the highest revenue, determining the busiest trading periods, analysing sales trends across products and time intervals, and providing strategic recommendations to improve overall business performance. The project combined data engineering, business intelligence, and data storytelling to produce insights that support data-driven decision-making at executive level. 

## Tools & Technologies Used

* **SQL** – Data extraction, transformation, KPI calculations, and business analysis
* **Databricks SQL** – Data cleaning, validation, feature engineering, and analytical queries
* **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard development and executive reporting
* **Google Data Studio (Looker Studio)** – Business dashboards, KPI reporting, and data visualisation
* **Lovable** – Designed and developed an **interactive executive dashboard** to present business insights, KPIs, and strategic recommendations in a user-friendly interface
* **Microsoft Excel** – Data validation, exploratory data analysis, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and quality assurance
* **Canva** – Executive presentation design, data storytelling, and **Gantt chart creation for project planning and timeline mapping**
* **Miro** – Brainstorming, project ideation, workflow planning, solution mapping, and data architecture design
* **Microsoft PowerPoint** – Development of the final executive presentation used to communicate business insights, strategic recommendations, and next steps to the CEO

## Project Workflow

The project followed a structured analytics methodology, beginning with project planning and solution design in **Miro**, where the data flow, ETL process, architecture, and analytical approach were mapped. The transactional sales data was converted, loaded into **Databricks**, and transformed using SQL to create new analytical fields, calculate revenue metrics, standardise data formats, and prepare reporting datasets. The processed data was then validated and explored in **Microsoft Excel**, where Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts were used to verify calculations and identify emerging trends. Interactive dashboards were subsequently developed using **Power BI**, **Google Data Studio**, and **Lovable** to present KPIs and business insights, while the final executive presentation was created in **PowerPoint** and enhanced with professional visuals designed in **Canva**.

## Business Outcomes

The project provides actionable insights to support revenue growth and operational improvement at Bright Coffee Shop. The analysis identified the highest-performing products, peak trading periods, sales trends across different time intervals, and opportunities to improve the performance of underperforming products. Based on these findings, strategic recommendations were developed, including targeted marketing campaigns during quieter trading periods, increased stock availability for best-selling items, promotion of lower-performing products, automation of sales reporting, multi-location performance tracking, and the future implementation of customer loyalty programmes. These recommendations directly support the CEO's objective of improving business performance through data-driven decision-making.
